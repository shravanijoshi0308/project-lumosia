# DrustiChuka ✨
*Even your Mac deserves a charm*
A good-luck charm that dangles on your Mac desktop.
The DrustiChuka is combination of a little Telugu chukka (dot) to catch the Marathi drusti (evil eye) before it reaches your Mac.
This a tiny playful charm that dangles on macOS over your desktop. 
You can pick your own charm that shows from a menu-bar icon.

**What and how it looks:** 
*Hangs one good-luck charm on your desktop, swaying like the real thing.*
*Lets you pick the charm from a menu-bar icon.*
*Shows only the selected charm — clean, never cluttered.*
*Is click-through — it floats on your wallpaper and never blocks anything.*
*Remembers your choice between launches.*

**Want your Charm? Follow these:**
Available from 3rd Implementation onward (once the desktop app lands). Until then, open index.html in a browser to see the charm prototype.

**The charms**
Nazar (evil eye) · Nimbu Mirchi · Evil Eye Beads · Hamsa Hand · Evil Eye Tassel — with more on the way. Adding one is a single line in the charms list plus its artwork.
